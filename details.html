Web Development Course

A Web Development course teaches students how to create, design, and maintain websites and web applications. It covers essential technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which are used to build the structure, style, and functionality of websites. Students may also learn responsive design, databases, APIs, version control, and popular frameworks such as React or Bootstrap. The course combines theoretical knowledge with practical projects, helping learners develop real-world skills. By completing projects, students gain experience in creating user-friendly and interactive websites. Web Development is a valuable skill for students, freelancers, and professionals because businesses increasingly depend on an online presence. Completing this course can open career opportunities such as web developer, front-end developer, back-end developer, or full-stack developer.
